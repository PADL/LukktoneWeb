Monitor Two Router One OCACON

OCACON

About

A universal controller for AES70 devices, with Milan, NMOS and Dante connection management support.

Networks
AES67, Milan, Dante
Control
AES70, NMOS
Platform
macOS
Availability
2027

Specifications

Features
  • Patch AES67, Milan, Dante and NMOS from a crosspoint matrix per protocol
  • Control any AES70 device, including Milan, Dante and NMOS devices represented by built-in proxies
  • User-built control panels
  • Network view of devices and the streams between them
Standards
  • AES70-2024 (OCA), based on the AES70-21, -22 and -23 connection management drafts
  • AES67 and SMPTE ST 2022-7
  • IEEE 1722.1 ATDECC
  • NMOS IS-04 and IS-05
